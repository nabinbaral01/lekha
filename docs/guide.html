1. What the system does: the one big idea

This is a cash-basis government accounting system that follows the म.ले.प. (Auditor General) formats. It replaces the paper books the office keeps today.

The golden rule: enter each transaction once. Every transaction is entered once, as a गोश्वारा भौचर (Form 203). When the office head approves it, the system writes it into every book automatically: the bank-cash book, the budget statement, the tax register, the income report, the dashboard charts and the audit log. No book is typed separately, so the books can never disagree with each other.

The four funds (कोष)

Money is kept in four separate funds. Every voucher belongs to exactly one fund.

पूँजीगत (Capital): assets, construction, purchasesचालु (Recurrent): operations, services, programmes
आन्तरिक (Internal): the committee's own revenueआन्तरिक – पूँजीगतआन्तरिक – चालु
अनुदान (Grant): money from government or other bodiesअनुदान – पूँजीगतअनुदान – चालु

The four funds share one real bank account. The system tracks each fund's share of it and never lets the total bank balance go below zero.

Main features at a glance

Entry

Form 203 voucher (easy payment mode or full debit/credit mode), income entry, tax deposit voucher, payee register, budget sub-heads.

Control

Approval workflow, budget check, bank-balance check, period locks, date-order numbering, duplicate-bill warning, role permissions.

Books

बैंक नगदी किताब (per fund and consolidated), हिसाब विवरण, TDS pending list, income by source, dashboards with charts.

Trust

Tamper-proof audit log (hash-chained and blocked from editing at database level), exact money (paisa, no rounding errors), Nepali/English, Nepali dates (BS).

6. Daily work: entering a payment voucher

Go to Home → आन्तरिक or अनुदान → पूँजीगत or चालु → भौचर प्रविष्टि. There are two modes.

A. सजिलो भुक्तानी (easy payment mode): used for almost everything

  1. Date (मिति) in BS, format YYYY.MM.DD. It must be inside the open fiscal year and in an unlocked month.
  2. Budget sub-head and project name (optional).
  3. खर्च शीर्षक (expense head): the list shows only heads of this fund's type (capital heads in पूँजीगत funds, recurrent heads in चालु funds) and the remaining budget next to each. The खाता पाना नं. and क्रियाकलाप नं. fill in automatically from the head.
  4. Payee (भुक्तानी पाउने): pick from the register or add a new one on the spot.
  5. Bill amount, bill number, bill date. Tick "बिलमा १३% मू.अ.कर समावेश छ" if the bill includes VAT. This is allowed only for VAT-registered payees.
  6. Tax type: the system calculates TDS and the net payment instantly (see chapter 8). To change the TDS, tick "TDS परिवर्तन गर्नुहोस्" and write a reason.
  7. Several payees can be added to one voucher.
  8. Payment method: EFT (needs the payee's bank account) or cheque.
  9. व्यहोरा (narration): a short description is required.
  10. Preview shows the exact Form 203 as it will print. Then save as draft or submit.

The system builds the accounting lines itself:

Dr  Expense head ............ bill amount (gross)
    Cr  Bank ................ net payment (bill − TDS)
    Cr  TDS payable ......... tax withheld

B. डेबिट/क्रेडिट (advanced mode)

For adjustments and unusual entries, the accountant writes each Dr/Cr line by hand, with the source columns (स्रोत व्यहोर्ने निकाय, भुक्तानी विधि). Total debit must equal total credit, and the screen shows the difference live.

Warnings you may see while entering

  • दोहोरो बिल हुनसक्छ (possible duplicate): the same payee and bill number already exist, or the same payee and amount on the same day. You must tick "मैले जाँचें, यो दोहोरो होइन" to continue.
  • Budget will be exceeded: shown at submit. Only the Super Admin can approve it, and only with a written reason, which is recorded.

Printing Form 203

Open the voucher and press प्रिन्ट. The page is an exact copy of the paper म.ले.प. फाराम नं. २०३ on one A4 sheet, including the उद्देश्य and फाराम भर्ने तरिका text, the amount in words, and the signature blocks with names and BS dates. Each print is recorded in the audit log.

What happens at each step

StepWhoWhat the system checks or does
Save draftAccountant / AdminAll structural rules (dates, heads, PAN, Dr = Cr, VAT rules, duplicate warning). A draft can be edited any number of times.
SubmitThe person who prepared it (or Admin)The month must be open. The budget check runs: if any head would go over budget it stops, unless the Super Admin gives a reason.
ApproveSuper Admin only 1. Only one approval at a time runs for the whole office, so numbers and balances never clash.
2. Date order: a voucher cannot be approved with a date earlier than the last approved voucher of that fund, so numbers stay in date order.
3. The budget check is repeated.
4. Bank check: the shared bank account (all four funds together) can never go negative.
5. For a tax deposit voucher, the amount must equal the TDS rows it clears.
6. Gives the next गो.भौ.नं. for that fund and year: sequential, gap-free, never reused.
7. From now on the voucher appears in every book.
RejectSuper AdminComment required. The voucher goes back to DRAFT with the comment shown at the top.
CancelPreparer / AdminOnly DRAFT or SUBMITTED. Reason required. An approved voucher can never be cancelled or edited; a correction is made with a new समायोजन (adjustment) voucher.
Mark paidAccountant / AdminRecords that the bank payment was actually made, with the EFT/cheque reference.
Two people editing the same voucher at once is detected: the second save is refused with "someone changed this voucher, reload" (version check), so no change is silently lost.

8. Tax deducted at source (TDS) and कर जम्मा

How the tax is calculated

  1. If the bill includes 13% VAT, the taxable base = bill ÷ 1.13. Otherwise the base = bill.
  2. TDS = base × rate of the chosen tax type. The rate used is the one in force on the voucher date.
  3. Rounding follows the office setting. The default is up to the next whole rupee, which matches the client's books.
  4. Net payment = bill − TDS.

All of this uses exact fractions, never decimals, so there is never a one-paisa error.

मू.अ.कर दर्ता नभएको फर्मको बिल (NON_VAT_FIRM)1.5%bill
व्यक्तिलाई सेवा/परामर्श शुल्क (INDIVIDUAL_SERVICE)15%bill
तलब/ज्याला सामाजिक सुरक्षा कर (SALARY_SST)1%bill

11. Monthly and year-end routine

Every day

  • Accountant enters and submits vouchers and income. The head approves or rejects them from the भौचर सूची (filter "पेस भएको"). After paying, mark vouchers भुक्तानी भएको.

Every month

  1. Check that no voucher of the month is still DRAFT or SUBMITTED.
  2. Deposit the month's TDS from the कर (TDS) tab.
  3. Print the month's cash book page and compare the bank balance with the bank statement.
  4. Lock the month (सेटिङ → अवधि बन्द). After locking, nobody can create, submit or approve a voucher dated in that month. Only the Super Admin can unlock it, and both actions are logged.

Year end (Ashadh end)

  1. Clear all drafts and pending vouchers. The year cannot be closed while any remain.
  2. Print the yearly cash book, हिसाब विवरण and audit log.
  3. Create the next fiscal year and enter its opening balance (the closing balance of this year).
  4. Close the old year.

13. Audit trail and security

  • Everything is logged: logins (including failed ones), creating, editing, submitting, approving, rejecting, cancelling and printing vouchers, and every settings change. Each entry records who, when, from which computer, and the data before and after.
  • Nobody can change the log. A database trigger blocks UPDATE, DELETE and TRUNCATE on the log table, even for the database administrator or a bug in the program.
  • Tamper check: each entry stores a fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of itself plus the previous entry, forming a chain. लेखाजोखा अभिलेख → जाँच गर्नुहोस् recomputes the chain and shows either "सुरक्षित छ" or the exact entry that was altered.
  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. Login tokens are stored as SHA-256 hashes, so a stolen database cannot be used to log in.
  • Auditor is read-only everywhere; the server refuses any change from that role.
  • Money is stored as whole paisa (BIGINT). There are no floating-point numbers anywhere, so totals are always exact.

Key design decisions

  • Books are derived, not stored. ledger.ts sums approved voucher lines on demand. Nothing can drift out of sync.
  • One compose() function builds the lines for preview, create and update, so what is previewed is exactly what is saved.
  • Money is BIGINT paisa, sent over JSON as exact decimal strings ("78050.00").
  • Approvals are serialised with a PostgreSQL advisory lock per office, and the voucher counter row is locked (FOR UPDATE), which gives gap-free numbers even with many users.
  • Optimistic locking: each voucher has a version number, and stale edits are refused.
  • BS dates are stored both as text (2082.04.01) and as an AD date for sorting and ranges.
